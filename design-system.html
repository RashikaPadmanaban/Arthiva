4. India-First Design Rules
Principle Implementation Example
Currency Grouping Indian lakhs and crores grouping, never western thousands ₹1,00,000, ₹15,000, ₹12.5L
Pay Cycle Logic All calculations run Payday-to-Payday (e.g. 1 Oct – 1 Nov or 30 Sep – 30 Oct), never calendar month 12 days to payday, Day 20 of 31
Commitment Types Preset chips match Indian salaried households with custom category expansion Rent, EMI, SIP, Insurance, Cook/Maid, Wi-Fi
Honest Self-Reporting Labeled as user-entered, never misleadingly "bank verified" "You entered · 2 days ago"
Privacy Pledge Zero SMS scraping, zero bank password harvesting Direct balance entry & audit log
